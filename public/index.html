AirDrop
for bills.

UPI solved payments. EZZ BILL handles what happens after.

Your spending lives in a dozen places: Gmail invoices, UPI texts, paper receipts, subscriptions that renew quietly. EZZ BILL pulls it in automatically and shows you where the money leaks.

NFC Airdrop · pilot

Tap the table. The bill is already on your phone.

Staff snap the closed bill and arm the table's NFC tag. The guest taps, the itemised bill opens on any phone and saves to EZZ BILL. No POS integration, and bills expire in minutes so nobody sees a stranger's receipt.

Zero-effort capture

Nobody types in expenses. So EZZ BILL doesn't ask you to.

Read-only Gmail import, UPI SMS parsing on Android, and AI receipt scanning that pulls out every line item. A merchant map tuned for India (Swiggy, Zepto, IRCTC, Jio) files it all automatically.

Gmail read-only OAuthGemini 2.5 FlashGPT-5-mini fallback
Money Leaks

See the gap between what you spend and what you should.

A six-question money profile builds a personal ideal budget for each category, compares it with real spending and shows the leak in rupees, plus a safe daily spend until payday.

Subscription radar

Find the subscriptions you forgot you pay for.

Recurring merchants are detected by cadence and amount, then marked active, low-use, unused or duplicate. Two music apps? You'll see the monthly saving before the next renewal.

Smart Split

Split it item by item. Settle it later.

Split any bill by line item or equally, with tax and discounts shared out proportionally. A friends ledger keeps track of who owes whom.

40-second walkthrough · live app, demo account

Business model

Merchant SaaS at ₹499–999 per location a month for digital bills and NFC Airdrop. Consumer premium at ₹49–99 a month; collecting bills stays free.

The pivot

Started as ExpenseDesk, a B2B reimbursement tool. Capture turned out to be the real pain, and individuals feel it daily, so we rebuilt for consumers.

Shipped

677+ commits on React, Capacitor and Supabase. NFC Airdrop, Gmail sync, AI bill reading, Money Leaks, warranties, subscriptions and splits are live.

Next

Play Store launch with the UPI SMS reader, Account Aggregator bank sync, and a restaurant partner rollout for NFC Airdrop.
